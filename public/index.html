Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the application and its distribution.

Is this app completely free to use?

Yes, 100% free forever. There are zero subscriptions, zero in-app purchases, and no advertisements. It is built as a pure devotional offering for the benefit of seekers worldwide.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. The entire scripture database — including all 18 chapters, 700 Sanskrit slokas, English word-for-word synonyms, translations, and complete purports — is packaged locally inside an SQLite database.

Which edition of the Bhagavad Gita is this?

This application faithfully presents Bhagavad-gītā As It Is by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, founder of ISKCON and the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust (BBT).

How do I install the APK on my Android device?

Download the APK file directly using the button above. When the download finishes, tap the notification or open your Downloads folder, tap the APK, and confirm installation when prompted by Android.

Can I customize the font size and colors?

Yes. Tap the "TT" icon on the bottom right of the reader screen to open preferences. You can smoothly slide font size from 12pt to 28pt and select between Parchment, Sepia, Paper, or Dark mode.

Is my reading progress saved automatically?

Yes. The app automatically remembers the exact chapter and verse you last visited. Tapping "Continue" on the home screen returns you instantly to your reading position.
